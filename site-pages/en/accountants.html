Working with your regnskapsfører

Arvaka takes over none of what your regnskapsfører (the accountant who keeps your books) does. We build on the accounts they keep, and help the managing director use them to look ahead.

Who does what

Your regnskapsfører Arvaka
Bookkeeping and vouchers Liquidity forecast and cash management
VAT and payroll reporting KPI tracking and budget against actuals
Year-end accounts and tax return Management report and board memo
The statutory responsibility for the accounts Early warnings and follow-up
Decision support: pricing, hiring, financing

When it is unclear who owns something, we use one rule: if it changes what the managing director should do this week, it is ours. If it is a filing with an authority, it belongs to the regnskapsfører.

We read, we do not post

Arvaka has read-only access to the accounting system. We cannot post vouchers, change entries or send anything on the company's behalf. This is not a setting that can be switched off: the tools that write are not open to us.

One clean-up list, not more questions

Good forecasts need accounts that add up. At onboarding, and then every month, we check that the accounts hang together: vouchers that have not been posted, customer and supplier ledgers that do not match the accounts, and whether the month has been closed. What we find is gathered in one clean-up list for the regnskapsfører, instead of questions trickling in through the month.

We assess the quality of the accounts because we cannot give advice on numbers that do not add up. But we never correct them ourselves.
