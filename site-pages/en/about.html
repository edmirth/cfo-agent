About

Most small and medium-sized businesses have a regnskapsfører, but nobody running management finance. Arvaka fills that gap.

Why we exist

The regnskapsfører keeps the books and looks backwards. What most businesses lack is someone looking forwards: someone who knows how low the bank balance will go in the coming weeks, which customers pay late, and what that means for the decisions the managing director has to make this week. That is a CFO's job, and for most businesses a CFO is too expensive to hire.

Arvaka connects to your accounts and delivers that work as finished output: a cash brief every Monday, warnings when something needs you, and decisions ready for approval.

How we work

  • Read-only access. We read your accounts, but cannot post, change or send anything in them.
  • The numbers are calculated in code. The AI explains what the numbers mean. Every number it writes is checked against its source.
  • Your regnskapsfører keeps their job. We do not keep the books or file any statutory reports. Anything we find that needs tidying up goes to the regnskapsfører.
