Data processing agreement

When Arvaka reads your accounts, we process personal data on your behalf. That is governed by a data processing agreement, as GDPR Article 28 requires. It is entered into before onboarding and applies for as long as you are a customer. The Norwegian version is the governing one.

The agreement is the EU's standard clauses

We use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors, Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915. They are part of the EEA Agreement and apply in Norway. We have changed nothing in them. What we decide ourselves are the choices the clauses let the parties make, a few additions that do not conflict with them, and the four annexes. All of that is on this page. In case of conflict, the standard clauses prevail.

Arvaka is being set up as a Norwegian limited company (AS). The agreement is signed with the company's name, organisation number and address once it is registered, and before we process data for any customer.

Choices in the standard clauses

  • Clause 5, docking clause: not included. The agreement is between the customer and Arvaka.
  • Clause 7.7, subprocessors: option 2, general written authorisation. The customer approves the subprocessors in Annex IV. We notify the customer at least 30 days before we engage a new subprocessor or replace one, and the customer can object to the change.

Additions

The standard clauses let the parties add terms that do not conflict with them. We have added:

  • Own purposes: We do not use the data for our own purposes, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.
  • Security breaches: We notify the customer without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after we became aware of the breach. We say what we know, and follow up with more when we know more.
  • Audits: The customer, or an auditor the customer has chosen, may audit us on 30 days' notice.
  • When the agreement ends: The accounts themselves are in the customer's accounting system and stay there. What we have stored, such as forecasts, cards, conversations and the customer's own description of the business, we delete within 30 days, including from backups, unless the customer has asked to receive it first.
  • Governing law: The agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes are settled by Oslo District Court (Oslo tingrett).

Annex I: The parties

Controller The customer: the business that uses Arvaka. Filled in on signing.
Processor Arvaka, kontakt@arvaka.no. The company's name, organisation number and address are filled in once the limited company is registered.

Annex II: The processing

What we do Read accounting data from the customer's accounting system (Tripletex) with read-only access, calculate forecasts and key figures, store the results, write analyses and answers with an AI model, and send the Monday brief by email.
Whom the data concerns The customer's employees, the customer's customers and suppliers who are individuals or sole proprietorships, contact persons, and the users of the service.
What data Names and contact details from the accounts. Invoices, payments and balances linked to customers and suppliers. Payroll as total cost and number of full-time equivalents; individual payslips are not read. Users' email addresses, messages in the chat and choices on cards.
Special categories The service is not designed to process special categories of personal data, such as health data. Voucher texts in the accounts may nevertheless contain such data. It is then processed only as part of the accounting data.
How often Continuously, for as long as the agreement applies: every working-day morning, and when users ask.
Where the data is stored The platform and database at Render in Frankfurt (EU). Login at Supabase in Stockholm (EU). Encrypted backups at Supabase in Ireland (EU).
For how long For as long as the agreement applies, and then until it is deleted under the addition on when the agreement ends.

Annex III: Security measures

  • Read-only access. Access to the accounting system is closed by default and opened one tool at a time, for reading only. No tools that write are available.
  • No individual payslips. The tools that read them are not opened.
  • Closed login. Only email addresses the customer has had added can get in. No one can sign up on their own. Every request to the server is checked.
  • Encrypted connections. All traffic between the browser, the server and the subprocessors goes over HTTPS.
  • Each customer separate. Each customer's data is in its own database, on its own disk.
  • Traceable runs. Every job and every call to the AI model is recorded with time and outcome.
  • Backups. Every night a copy of the customer's database is taken and checked before it is stored. Copies are kept for 14 days on the server's disk, and for 14 days encrypted at Supabase in Ireland. The key that opens them is kept separate from the copies.
  • Access at Arvaka. Only Arvaka's founder has access to the operating environment, the databases and the backups, with a personal login.
