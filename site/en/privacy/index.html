Privacy policy

This page explains what personal data Arvaka processes, why we do it, who helps us, and what rights you have. The Norwegian version is the governing one.

Who is responsible

Arvaka is the controller for the data described here. Questions about privacy go to personvern@arvaka.no. Arvaka is being set up as a Norwegian limited company (AS); the organisation number and address will appear here once it is registered.

When we read a customer's accounts, the customer is the controller and we are the processor. That is governed by the data processing agreement.

When you visit arvaka.no

  • We use no cookies, no analytics tools and no tracking.
  • Fonts and images are loaded from arvaka.no. Your browser contacts no one else when you visit the site.
  • Our hosting provider, Render, gives us no logs of visits to the website.

When you get in touch or book a call

We process your name, your email address and what you write to us, in order to reply. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our email and our booking page are provided by Google (Google Workspace), which processes name, email and time on our behalf. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

If no agreement follows, we delete the enquiry after 12 months.

When you use the platform

If your company has an agreement with us and you have been given access, we process:

  • Your login: your email address and password. The password is not stored in plain text. Login is handled by Supabase, with storage in Stockholm.
  • What you write and decide: messages in the chat and the choices you make on cards are stored, so that Arvaka remembers them between conversations.
  • The Monday brief: sent by email to the addresses your company has chosen, through Resend.
  • Time in the app and taps from the Monday brief, only if you have said yes: while the app is open and in focus, we record one signal a minute. If you come into the app from a link in the Monday brief, we record which brief it was, and whether the link went to the brief, a card or the list of cards. We do not record what you read, and the email has no invisible images that track whether it is opened. We use it to understand how much time the service takes, and to improve it. You are asked the first time you log in, and can change your mind at any time under Selskapet (Company) in the app. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a)).
  • Technical logs: the server logs requests, with IP address, time and which address was fetched, for operations and security. We do not use them to follow individuals.

For the rest, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the service your company has an agreement for (Article 6(1)(f)).

The accounting data

Arvaka reads the accounts with read-only access. Accounting data can contain personal data, such as the names of customers and suppliers who are individuals. We process it only to deliver the service, on the customer's instructions. The AI model we use is provided by Anthropic, which under its agreement with us may not train models on the data. Everything about this is in the data processing agreement, including which subprocessors we use and where they process the data.

Who we share with

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for marketing. We share it only with the subprocessors that help us deliver the service, listed in the data processing agreement. Some of them process data in the USA; the agreement says on what basis.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an agreement: 12 months.
  • User accounts: as long as the company has an agreement with us, and deleted 30 days after the agreement ends.
  • Technical logs: deleted automatically after at most 14 days.

Your rights

You can ask for access to the data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also ask us to restrict processing, to receive the data in a common format, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to personvern@arvaka.no, and we will reply within 30 days.

If you believe we process data in breach of the rules, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).
