Built for how your company earns money.

Recurring contracts and project work move cash in very different ways. Arvaka forecasts both.

Contract mode

Recurring revenue

Service contracts, retainers and subscriptions with fixed monthly invoices. We track payroll ratio and margin per contract, and apply index clauses when they renew.

Project mode

Project-based work

A-konto invoices, retention, long payment terms and costs paid up front. We show you February's cash in September.
